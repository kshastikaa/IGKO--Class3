## 🎮 How to Play

1. ✏️ **Enter** your name and school name.
2. 🚀 **Tap** the orange **Start Practice Test** button.
3. 🔘 **Choose** the right answer: A, B, C or D.
4. 🎊 **Press Submit** to see your score and enjoy the confetti!

Answered questions turn **green** in the side drawer, so you always know where you are.
